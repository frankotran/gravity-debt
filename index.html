HOW TO PLAY
TAP / SPACE — flip gravity. Every flip costs 1 energy.
Gold orbs recharge 1 flip (+5 pts). Run dry and you're stuck.

Combo — chain tricks within 4s to build up to x5 score.
Bounce pads — hit one mid-flip to spring back to your side.
Volatile orbs — red orbs explode, vaporizing nearby obstacles.
Mystery orbs — rare ? orbs with random jackpots.

Powerups — shield eats one hit · magnet vacuums orbs · overdrive doubles score · bullet time slows everything.
